# Method Schedule 0.8.0

Method Schedule puts more on the call sheet, including crew calls by department and how to reach each location, and lets you tell Suggest Strip Order what matters most on your shoot.

## Call sheets with crew and directions

- **Crew calls by department.** Set each department's call once against the company's, such as an hour and a half before it. Every day's call sheet lists them, printed and in its data files.
- **Crew calls for one day.** In the Day Timeline, give a department a different call on one day, call a department that day alone, or type `off` to leave it out of that day.
- **How to reach each location.** Plan > Locations has a Details button for an address, directions and a map picture you choose. The call sheet lists each of the day's locations with these and its coordinates. Method Schedule doesn't fetch maps from the internet, so your schedule stays on your computer.
- **Clocks set by a lunar calendar or a decree.** Where a location's clocks change on dates a rule can't work out, type the year's dates and their UTC offset under Details. Sunrise and sunset use them on those dates.

## Strip orders that follow your priorities

- **Weights you set.** Suggest Strip Order has a Weights button. Say what an actor's day, a location change, a switch between day and night, a red flag clash and an hour past the long day each cost. They stay with the schedule.
- **Long days count.** Each hour a day runs past your long day limit now adds to an order's cost, so a suggestion can even out heavy days.
- **More orders tried.** After the usual orders, it swaps pairs of scenes while that lowers the cost. The same board always gives the same suggestion. It finds a good order, not always the best one.

## Script drafts on the board

- **A draft's color on its scenes.** A small swatch at the start of a strip shows the newest draft that changed the scene, on the board and when printed. A strip keeps its own colors.
- **Your own color names.** Plan > Script Drafts can rename a draft's color. The next revision skips any name already in use.

## Working together and from the command line

- **Conflict copies offered for merging.** When a shared folder leaves a conflict copy beside your file, Method Schedule offers to merge it.
- **Going to a change from inside a merge.** Open the sheet or element a change is about, then come back with your picks kept.
- **More from the command line and the MCP server.** Crew calls, offset dates, directions, order weights and draft color names can all be set there too.

## Fixed

- The Red Flag Types window no longer shows the word "null" when no standard type is missing.

## Known gaps

- A schedule that uses crew calls, a location's own offset dates or directions, or its own order weights opens in 0.8.0 and later, not in 0.7.0 or earlier.
- The map on a call sheet is the picture you choose. It prints in gray, and a missing picture prints as "Map not found" with its path.
- On an Intel Mac, the app has been tested by the automated tests, run the way an Intel Mac runs them, but not yet by a person on one.
- Windows and Linux builds are made and tested automatically, but nobody has installed them by hand yet. Not signed, and no certificate is planned: Windows warns that the publisher is unknown before it opens.
- On Windows and Linux, opening a second file while the app is running starts a second copy of the app.
- Printing uses Letter paper only.
