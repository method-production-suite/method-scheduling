# Method Schedule 0.2.0

A whole invented feature to learn on, ready the moment the app opens, and downloads you can check before you install them.

## Field Station, a full feature to try

![The stripboard of Field Station: the first days of an 18-day shoot, with scenes, banners and day breaks](../../assets/screens/board-light.png)

- **Help > Open Sample opens Field Station.** An invented feature of 90 scenes and nine cast, one of whom never speaks, scheduled over 18 shoot days at three locations with a company move to a lake for three days.
- **Every part of the app has something in it.** Every breakdown category is filled, with background counts. There are lunch and company move banners, call times, a second unit for the drive on the fire road, prep and wrap on the calendar, and red flags, one of them a soft clash the board marks.
- **It opens once on its own.** The first time Method Schedule starts with nothing to open, it shows you Field Station, untitled, with a line saying what it is. It never does again: open it any time from the Help menu. If you open your own schedule first, or you used an earlier version, you won't see it unasked.

![The day out of days for Field Station: each cast member's work days across the shoot](../../assets/screens/dood-light.png)

## Downloads you can check

- **A checksum for every download.** Each release now comes with a `SHA256SUMS` file listing every installer and update file with its SHA-256, so you can check a download is the one we published before you install it.

## For other apps and the command line

- **Two switches to start with.** `--check-for-updates` checks for a newer version as the app opens, and `--open-sample` opens Field Station, for a launcher or a shortcut.
- **The recent files list is easy to read.** Open Recent's list is kept as a plain list of files, each with when it was last opened, so other tools can show your recent schedules.
- **Room for what was shot.** A schedule file can now hold what was actually shot on each day, and how much of each scene, for a production report to write back. The app doesn't show it yet.

## Fixed

- **A speech in two paragraphs keeps its length.** In a Fountain script, the second paragraph of a speech was counted as action, which made a scene's length come out a line or two short.

## Known gaps

- Windows and Linux builds are made automatically, but nobody has installed them yet. The Windows installer is unsigned, so Windows shows a warning before it runs.
- Printing uses Letter paper only.
- What was shot isn't shown on the board or in the reports yet.
- A schedule that holds what was shot won't open in 0.1.0: update first.
